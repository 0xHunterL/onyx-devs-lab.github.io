# Hong Kong enterprise AI governance: an implementation checklist

Hong Kong organisations do not need to turn every AI pilot into a compliance programme. They do need a control boundary that matches the people, data, decisions, and consequences involved.

The Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD) groups its Model Personal Data Protection Framework into four areas:

1. AI strategy and governance;
2. risk assessment and human oversight;
3. model customisation, system implementation, and management;
4. communication and engagement with stakeholders.

An implementation team can turn those areas into three inspectable artefacts:

- a **data and purpose register** recording the intended use, personal data, provenance, retention, access, cross-border movement, and prohibited uses;
- a **decision and authority map** separating advice, drafting, approval, and execution, with a named owner and escalation route;
- a **release and monitoring record** containing realistic test cases, risk-tier thresholds, failures, mitigations, approvals, model and system versions, drift signals, incidents, and review dates.

Human oversight is not a disclaimer. It must give the reviewer enough information and time to intervene, a real route to override or stop the system, and an escalation path that matches the consequence of error.

# 香港企业 AI 治理：实施清单

香港企业不需要把每个 AI 试点都变成庞大的合规项目，但必须根据涉及的人员、数据、决策和错误后果建立相应控制边界。

香港隐私专员公署的《人工智能（AI）：个人资料保障示范框架》包含四个范围：

1. AI 战略与治理；
2. 风险评估与人为监督；
3. 模型定制、系统实施及管理；
4. 与利益相关者沟通和交流。

实施团队可以把这些原则转化成三类可核验材料：

- **数据和用途登记**：记录预定用途、个人数据、数据来源、保留期限、访问权限、跨境流动和禁止用途；
- **决策与权限图**：区分建议、草拟、批准和执行，为每项高影响操作指定负责人和升级路径；
- **发布和监控记录**：保留真实测试案例、风险门槛、失败与缓解、批准、模型和系统版本、漂移信号、事故及复核日期。

人为监督不等于增加一条免责声明。复核人员必须有足够的信息和时间介入，能够真正否决或停止系统，并拥有与错误后果匹配的升级路径。
