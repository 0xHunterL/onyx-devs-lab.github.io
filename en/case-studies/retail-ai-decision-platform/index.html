Turning retail transaction data into operating decisions

A non-invasive analytics and AI layer designed around an existing SaaS ERP for an Italian-Chinese supermarket operation.

By Onyx Devs Lab · Published 2026-09-07 · Updated 2026-09-11

繁體中文简体中文

The operating challenge

Transaction data existed, but inventory, workforce, and loss signals were fragmented. The business needed better decisions without replacing the operational ERP.

Designed intervention

01

Connected existing transaction data through a separate analytics layer.

02

Designed missing workforce and inventory-loss data modules.

03

Applied inventory forecasting and market-basket analysis to operational decisions.

04

Kept recommendations reviewable by operators rather than automating high-impact decisions invisibly.
