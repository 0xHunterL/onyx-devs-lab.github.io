Legal AI that returns every conclusion to its evidence

A legal operations and dossier-analysis system designed around document coverage, page-level citations, matter permissions, and accountable lawyer review.

By Onyx Devs Lab · Published 2026-09-07 · Updated 2026-09-10

繁體中文简体中文

The operating challenge

Law-firm operations and large case files created two different information problems: fragmented business status and high-risk evidence retrieval across very large dossiers. A fluent answer was not enough; lawyers needed coverage, provenance, and explicit review.

Designed intervention

01

Connected leads, clients, matters, milestones, tasks, contracts, and receivables in one operating model.

02

Tracked ingestion by file, page, and text chunk so missing or failed material remained visible.

03

Combined keyword and vector retrieval with file, page, source text, and confidence coordinates.

04

Applied matter-level access, audit trails, exception queues, and lawyer approval before formal output.
