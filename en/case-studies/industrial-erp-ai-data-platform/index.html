A trusted data path from industrial operations to management decisions

An ERP and AI information layer connecting weighbridge, laboratory, vehicle, energy, and PLC data while keeping operational technology control separate.

繁體中文简体中文

The operating challenge

Production information was fragmented across equipment, paper records, and isolated systems. Management received delayed summaries, but connecting the data could not compromise safety-related industrial control.

Designed intervention

01

Mapped material movement, weighing, quality testing, vehicle energy, and production records to accountable business objects.

02

Established a controlled data interface between PLC information and management systems.

03

Unified operational dashboards, mobile access, permissions, validation, and audit records.

04

Added business questions and exception explanation above the trusted data layer, not direct AI control of production.
