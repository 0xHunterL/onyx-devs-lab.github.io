## What we build

Custom development is appropriate when AI must operate inside a specific workflow or data boundary.

### AI agents

Tool-using agents with explicit permissions, evidence, escalation, and human approval.

### Knowledge systems

RAG and document intelligence with retrieval evaluation, citations, and access control.

### Operational automation

Reliable workflows that combine deterministic rules, AI judgement, queues, retries, and audit trails.

## Production requirements

A demonstration is not a production system.

### Evaluation

Representative test sets, accuracy criteria, regression checks, and failure analysis.

### Security and control

Least-privilege access, tenant boundaries, sensitive-data handling, and action confirmation.

### Operations

Monitoring, cost visibility, fallbacks, documentation, training, and accountable ownership.

## Delivery method

### Specify

Confirm users, workflow, data, integrations, and acceptance criteria.

### Prototype

Resolve uncertain model and interaction questions with working software.

### Build

Implement the system, integrations, controls, evaluation, and observability.

### Launch

Pilot with real users, correct failure modes, train operators, and hand over.

## Frequently asked questions

How much does custom AI development cost?

Cost depends on integration depth, data condition, risk controls, evaluation needs, and operating requirements. We scope the system after diagnosis rather than price only by screen count or model calls.

Do you build on a specific model provider?

No. We select models and infrastructure based on quality, latency, privacy, availability, and cost requirements.

Can AI integrate with an ERP that has limited APIs?

Often yes. Options include supported APIs, database replicas, scheduled exports, event streams, or controlled interface automation. The safest route depends on the ERP and permitted access.
