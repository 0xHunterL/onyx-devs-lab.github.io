# Make the right AI decision before you build.

Onyx helps leadership, operations, and technology teams identify where AI can create measurable value, what data and controls it requires, and how to move from decision to a production system.

[Book a project assessment](mailto:info@onyxdevslab.com?subject=Make%20the%20right%20AI%20decision%20before%20you%20build.) [繁體中文](/zh-hk/ai-consulting/) [简体中文](/zh-cn/ai-consulting/)

**Senior-only** — Strategy and engineering in one team

**Build-aware** — Advice grounded in production delivery

## What the engagement answers

An AI advisory engagement should end in decisions, not a collection of trends.

### Opportunity diagnosis

Map workflows, constraints, users, and decision points before selecting a model or platform.

### Feasibility and risk

Assess data readiness, integration limits, privacy, permissions, failure modes, and human review.

### Delivery roadmap

Prioritise use cases by value, evidence, implementation effort, and operational risk.

## Typical deliverables

The scope is adapted to the decision you need to make.

### Decision memo

A concise recommendation, assumptions, rejected options, and investment boundary.

### Opportunity map

A ranked portfolio of workflows with owners, dependencies, and measurable outcomes.

### 90-day roadmap

A phased plan covering prototype, pilot, production controls, adoption, and validation.

## Delivery method

### Diagnose

Interview operators, inspect workflows, and establish the current baseline.

### Frame

Define the business decision, data boundary, risks, and acceptance criteria.

### Validate

Test the highest-risk assumptions with the smallest credible experiment.

### Plan

Produce a build, integration, governance, and operating route.

## Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between AI consulting and AI development?

Consulting determines what should be built, why, and within which constraints. Development turns a defined need into an operating system. Onyx can deliver either independently or connect both phases.

Can you assess an existing AI project?

Yes. We can review architecture, model quality, data access, operational controls, adoption, cost, and whether the project has a credible production path.

Do you only work with Hong Kong companies?

No. The Hong Kong site is designed for Hong Kong and cross-border engagements; delivery can support distributed teams and international operations.

## Start with one concrete operating problem.

We will first clarify the workflow, evidence, data boundary, and outcome standard before recommending the next step.
