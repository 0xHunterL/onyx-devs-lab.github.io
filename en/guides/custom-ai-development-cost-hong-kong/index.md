# What determines custom AI development cost in Hong Kong?

A credible estimate is driven less by the number of screens than by integration depth, data condition, evaluation, permissions, and the operating responsibility the system must carry.

By [Onyx Devs Lab](/en/about/) · Published 2026-09-07 · Updated 2026-09-11

**Scope before price** — Define the operating boundary

**Risk before features** — Test expensive unknowns early

**TCO, not demo cost** — Include operation and ownership

## The main cost drivers

Two interfaces that look similar can have very different production requirements.

### Integration depth

Supported APIs are different from legacy exports, browser-mediated systems, identity integration, and bidirectional actions.

### Evidence and evaluation

High-trust work requires representative test sets, citations, error analysis, regression checks, and human-review rules.

### Operational controls

Queues, retries, permissions, audit logs, monitoring, fallbacks, and support often determine whether a prototype can go live.

## What a useful estimate contains

A single headline price without assumptions cannot support a procurement decision.

### Defined workflow

Named users, inputs, outputs, exceptions, integrations, and consequential actions.

### Delivery stages

Separate discovery, prototype, pilot, production hardening, training, and ongoing operation.

### Acceptance and change rules

State measurable acceptance criteria, excluded scope, dependencies, and how new evidence changes the plan.

## Delivery method

### Map one workflow

Avoid estimating an undefined enterprise-wide AI programme.

### Expose the unknowns

List data, API, permission, accuracy, latency, and adoption risks.

### Price a validation stage

Resolve assumptions that could invalidate the architecture or business case.

### Estimate production ownership

Include security, evaluation, monitoring, support, and handover.

## Frequently asked questions

Can you provide a fixed price?

A fixed price can be responsible after the workflow, dependencies, and acceptance criteria are bounded. Earlier work is usually better structured as a defined assessment or validation stage.

Why is an AI prototype cheaper than production deployment?

A prototype demonstrates a capability. Production deployment must manage real permissions, failures, edge cases, observability, data handling, and accountable human intervention.

How can a buyer reduce cost risk?

Start with one measurable workflow, give early access to representative data and operators, and agree on stop or redesign conditions before full implementation.
